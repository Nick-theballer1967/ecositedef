Building performance monitoring

MyPortal 2.0 — Greater Visibility Into Your Building Performance

Reducing building energy consumption is only part of the story. Understanding how buildings are performing, verifying savings and identifying opportunities for further improvement are equally important.

EcoAI delivers dynamic, real-time HVAC optimisation through existing Building Management Systems, continuously adjusting operation to reduce unnecessary energy consumption without adding to engineers' workloads.

Alongside this optimisation, Myrspoven's MyPortal provides a clear view of building performance, helping building owners, estates teams and facilities managers better understand the results being delivered.

The latest MyPortal 2.0 update introduces an improved interface, clearer portfolio visibility and enhanced tools for reviewing building performance.

More Than Energy Savings

Dynamic HVAC optimisation and clear performance information work together to deliver a stronger operational and commercial proposition.

Continuous Energy Optimisation

myCoreAI works alongside existing BMS infrastructure, dynamically optimising HVAC operation every 15 minutes using live building data and forecast conditions.

Measured and Verifiable Results

Understand the energy savings being delivered and use performance information to support operational, financial and sustainability reporting.

Portfolio-Wide Visibility

For organisations managing multiple buildings, MyPortal provides a consolidated view of performance, helping teams identify patterns, compare results and prioritise attention where it is most valuable.

Supporting Engineering Teams

Better visibility combined with automated optimisation helps reduce repetitive manual intervention, allowing engineering teams to concentrate on maintenance, operational issues and higher-value activities.

Proven Savings in Real Buildings

At the University of St Andrews, EcoAI's myCoreAI optimisation delivered a verified 21.1% reduction in HVAC electricity consumption and a 9.2% reduction in heat consumption at the Scottish Oceans Institute.

The results demonstrate how meaningful energy savings can be achieved through existing BMS infrastructure, without replacing the building's control system.
