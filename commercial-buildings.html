Commercial buildings

HVAC optimisation for commercial buildings and portfolios

EcoAI helps commercial buildings reduce HVAC energy through existing BMS infrastructure, without replacing plant, disrupting tenants or relying on ongoing manual tuning.

For offices, multi-let buildings, landlords, managing agents, asset managers and FM teams, HVAC performance affects operating costs, tenant comfort, asset value and portfolio reporting. EcoAI helps improve those outcomes while retaining the BMS infrastructure already in place.

Commercial fit

EcoAI is relevant where existing commercial buildings have suitable BMS access, meaningful HVAC loads and controllable setpoints.

  • Tenant comfort Reduce wasted HVAC energy while protecting agreed comfort limits and day-to-day operations.
  • Operating cost and NOI Lower avoidable HVAC runtime to support reduced costs and stronger net operating income.
  • Portfolio reporting Evidence energy, carbon and performance improvements without replacing existing infrastructure.

The commercial building challenge

Comfort, cost and carbon all have to be managed at once

Tenant comfort

Commercial buildings need to reduce energy without creating comfort complaints, lease friction or operational disruption.

Changing occupancy

Office use, weather conditions and internal gains can change throughout the week, making fixed schedules and static settings inefficient.

Operating cost and NOI pressure

Owners and operators need measurable reductions in consumption, cost and carbon that can support asset performance and net operating income.

Portfolio reporting

Asset managers and property teams need clearer evidence of savings, comfort performance and carbon reduction across suitable buildings.
