Executive summary

Operational optimisation alongside estate decarbonisation

A UK local authority was implementing a wider building decarbonisation programme across its estate, including heat pumps, solar generation, battery storage and Building Management System improvements.

At an 8,500 m² civic office, EcoAI's optimisation technology was integrated with the existing BMS to improve HVAC operation while retaining the building's installed controls infrastructure.

The project demonstrates how active HVAC/BMS optimisation can work alongside wider decarbonisation investment without requiring the existing controls platform to be replaced.

Assessment approach

Why the expected post-project profile mattered

The challenge was not simply to compare one year with another. The wider carbon-reduction measures were expected to materially change the building's energy profile, with electricity consumption forecast to increase by 35% and gas consumption forecast to reduce by 70%.

Performance was therefore assessed against the expected post-project/post-decarbonisation profile. Across the first 12 months, electricity consumption was 222,278 kWh below expected. Gas consumption was 84,577 kWh above the expected level, giving a net energy reduction of 137,701 kWh.

At the energy rates used in the first-year analysis, this equated to a calculated net saving of £37,474.87.

Demand shift

Optimisation measured against the future operating profile

Heat electrification and other carbon-reduction projects can shift demand between fuels. Optimisation performance therefore needs to be considered against the expected future operating profile rather than only a historic baseline.

EcoAI's contribution was evaluated against the adjusted expected profile to separate the optimisation effect from the wider programme as far as the available data allowed.

What the project shows

Three practical lessons for decarbonising estates

Existing BMS

Optimisation can be introduced through existing controls infrastructure rather than requiring wholesale replacement.

Wider programme

Operational optimisation can complement heat pumps, on-site generation, storage and other decarbonisation measures.

Measured value

The business case can be evaluated against an adjusted expected profile when other projects are changing the building's energy mix.
